How to play

Crystalbingo is a challenge to complete the bingo card. Choose Timer or No Timer, then a random player appears at the top and you must match them to 1 of the 16 categories on the card. More players appear until you complete the card or run out of players.

If Cristiano Ronaldo appears and the card has Ballon d'Or or Juventus, think carefully: you can only pick 1 per player.

Categories include clubs, countries, regions, leagues, trophies, awards, managers and teammates. Half the players fit nothing on the card, so skipping is part of the game.

Pick a wrong category and you miss the next player. Skip safely with the Skip button to avoid the penalty.

If a player fits several categories, play your wildcard to fill them all. You get it once per card.

Fewer players used means a higher efficiency score. A new card is waiting at midnight. Use Yesterday or the arrows under the board to play older cards, or play the same card as your friends.
